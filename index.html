The M39 Swedish Rucksack

These handmade packs were introduced in 1939, at the start of WWII. The design has simple, rugged lines, solid construction and classic looks. It's been called virtually indestructible, and it holds a lot: books, camping gear, or a mini bar for a deserted beach.

Features

  • External steel frame
  • Khaki/green canvas main pack
  • Interior small-items pocket
  • 550 cord drawstring top
